Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026. This is a plain-language description of what the site stores and who processes it. It is not legal advice.

Who is responsible

Animesh, an individual developer. Contact: aanimesh_be23@thapar.edu.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (completed lessons, XP, streak, badges), notes, bookmarks, AI tutor conversations for each lesson, and settings (theme, level, daily goal, sidebar mode) are saved in your browser's localStorage. The site also caches its own files in your browser so it works offline. None of this leaves your device unless you sign in or use the features below.

No analytics, no ads

The site does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

Optional sign-in and sync

If you sign in (email and password, Google or GitHub), Firebase Authentication handles the sign-in. Your progress, notes, bookmarks and settings are then copied to Cloud Firestore under your account so they follow you between devices. You can use the whole course without doing this.

The AI tutor

The tutor is for signed-in learners. When you ask a question, the browser sends your question, the recent messages of that conversation, your chosen level and the text of the lesson you have open (and the code, if you ask about a code block) to a function hosted on Vercel. That function checks your sign-in and forwards the request to Groq, which generates the answer. The server does not store your questions or the answers. It stores only counters in Firestore (how many questions your account asked today, and a site-wide daily count) to apply the daily limit. Do not type personal or secret information into the tutor.

The Run button

If you press Run on a code sample, the code and any input you typed are sent to the Wandbox online compiler (wandbox.org) to be compiled and run. Nothing is sent unless you press Run. Do not put secrets in the code.

Who processes data

  • Firebase and Google (Authentication and Cloud Firestore): sign-in and optional sync.
  • Groq: generates AI tutor answers.
  • Wandbox: compiles and runs code when you press Run.
  • Vercel: hosts the site and runs the AI tutor function. Hosts like this keep standard server logs, such as IP address and request time.

Each has its own privacy policy. If you sign in with Google or GitHub, that provider tells Firebase your name, email and profile picture.

Deleting your data

  • On this device: Settings has Reset progress, and you can clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove everything.
  • In the cloud: when you are signed in, Settings has Delete cloud data, which removes your synced copy from Firestore. Data on your device is kept.
  • Your account: there is no delete-account button yet. Email aanimesh_be23@thapar.edu from the address you signed up with and I will delete your sign-in account and your usage counters.

Children

The site is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes and governing law

I may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. It is governed by the laws of India. See also the Terms of use.
